Meet Tatiana Grant

Faith. Wellness. Purpose. Growth.

I am a woman of faith, a proud mother, a serial entrepreneur, and a domestic violence survivor who believes that every chapter of life, both the beautiful and the difficult, has the power to shape who we are becoming. As a businesswoman, I've spent years building brands, 7-figure businesses, and opportunities for others. But some of my greatest lessons have come through life's most challenging seasons. I've learned that success isn't just about what we accomplish, but about who we become along the way. My journey has taught me the importance of faith, resilience, wellness, intentional living, and giving myself the grace to grow. Most importantly, I've learned that I am blessed to have this life, blessed to still be here, and blessed with the opportunity to keep becoming the best version of myself.

That's the heart behind Blessed to Best, a lifestyle rooted in gratitude, guided by purpose, and committed to growth. Through faith, wellness, entrepreneurship, and personal development, I hope to inspire others to embrace their journeys, recognize their blessings, and pursue their best selves—one intentional day at a time. Because being blessed doesn't mean life has been perfect. It means recognizing the gift of life, the strength to keep going, and the opportunity to become more.
