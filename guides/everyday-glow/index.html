An everyday glow routine with cleansing, mist, body glow and highlighter

A simple beauty routine featuring cleansing balm, Glow Face Mist, CC Body Lotion and Highlighter Stick.

Important: This guide organizes the usage directions supplied for the products. Always follow the current product label and Farmasi product page.

Start with cleansing when needed

Apply Cleansing Balm to dry skin with fingertips, gently massage and rinse well with warm water. Continue with your normal skincare routine.

Refresh with face mist

Close your eyes and hold Glow Face Mist 20–30 cm from your face. Mist evenly over bare skin or use it over makeup when you want a refreshed finish.

Body glow

Apply CC Body Lotion to clean, dry body skin. Massage to activate the encapsulated pigments, blend evenly and allow it to set before dressing. The supplied product information says to avoid the face.

Finish with highlighter

Swipe Highlighter Stick onto the high points of the face or other areas you want to illuminate, then blend. Build in thin layers.
