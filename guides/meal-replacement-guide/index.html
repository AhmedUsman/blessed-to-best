Exploring meal replacement shakes.

A label-first comparison of Nutriplus shake flavors and the nutritional facts to check before choosing a meal replacement.

What to compare

Check the serving size, protein amount, calories, allergens, added ingredients and preparation instructions on each package. Meal replacements are one nutrition format; they do not replace a balanced diet or individualized guidance. Compare flavors based on preference rather than assuming different flavors have identical formulations.
