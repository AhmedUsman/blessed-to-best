Tea Tree skincare routine: product order and directions

A simple label-based Tea Tree routine using the face wash, toner, serum and cream balsam.

Important: This guide organizes the usage directions supplied for the products. Always follow the current product label and Farmasi product page.

1. Cleanse

Use the Tea Tree Face Wash morning and night. Apply to damp hands, work into a lather, massage over damp face and neck while avoiding the eye area, then rinse thoroughly.

2. Tone

After cleansing, moisten a cotton pad with Tea Tree Face Toner and gently apply to the skin, avoiding the eye area. The supplied directions say to use it morning and night.

3. Apply serum where needed

Apply a few drops of Tea Tree Serum directly to needed areas on clean skin. The supplied directions allow morning-and-night or as-needed use.

4. Finish with cream balsam

Apply Tea Tree Cream Balsam to cleansed skin and gently massage in circular motions until fully absorbed. Use daily or as needed.

Where the soap fits

Tea Tree Soap is listed for face and body use. Apply to wet skin, rinse thoroughly and dry with a clean towel. It can be treated as a separate cleansing option rather than adding another mandatory step to the facial routine.
