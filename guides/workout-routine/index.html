Pre-workout, creatine and hydration: label-based timing

A simple workout-day guide based on the supplied directions for pre-workout, creatine, electrolytes and L-carnitine.

Important: This guide organizes the usage directions supplied for the products. Always follow the current product label and Farmasi product page.

Before training

The supplied Pre-Workout directions say to mix with cold water and drink 20–30 minutes before training. The product contains caffeine, so review all label precautions before use.

Hydration

The supplied Electrolyte Drink Mix directions recommend dissolving 2 effervescent tablets in 500 ml of water once a day for adults.

After training

The supplied Creatine directions say to mix 2 scoops (5.1 g) with 100 ml of water or a preferred beverage and consume after your workout.

L-Carnitine timing

The supplied L-Carnitine Shot directions say to consume one shot daily before or after workouts.

Supplement note: Do not combine products or change serving amounts beyond their current labels without considering total ingredients such as caffeine and your own health circumstances.
