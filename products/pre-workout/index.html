How to use

Mix with cold water and drink 20–30 minutes before training. Men: 5 scoops (20 g) with 300–450 ml water. Women: 2.5 scoops (10 g) with 200–300 ml water. First-time users should start with a lower amount to assess tolerance.

Formula and ingredients

Citrulline malate (2:1), beta-alanine, betaine anhydrous, taurine, L-tyrosine, caffeine, sodium chloride, citric acid, maltodextrin, natural flavors, beet root extract, sunflower lecithin, stevia.

Important product notes

For healthy adults 18+. Contains caffeine. Do not use if pregnant, nursing or sensitive to caffeine. Review the full precautions on the product label.
